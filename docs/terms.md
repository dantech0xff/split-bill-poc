## What Split Vibe does

Split Vibe helps you split a bill and ask people for their share. You photograph or scan a bill, or type it in, and say how it should be split. The phone reads the text of the bill, and an AI model works out the bill's items, charges and total from that text. The app then works out each person's share, which you can check and change. You can also change a split by typing or saying a sentence, keep bills in groups with balances, open a bill for everyone at the table to pick their items, and send each person a payment request with the way you want to be paid.

Typing a bill in by hand, splitting it, groups, payment requests, insights, exports and recurring bills are free. The AI features count against a number of AI calls:

- without signing in, each install of the app gets 3 calls, once;
- signed in with a Google account, you get 10 calls each calendar month, counted in UTC;
- with Pro, you get 300 calls each calendar month instead of 10.

Reading a bill takes one call, and changing a split by a sentence takes a fifth of one. The app shows how many calls you have left.

To keep the service working and affordable, our server also limits how many AI calls can come from one network address and caps what the AI may cost in a day. When a limit is reached, the AI may be unavailable for a while, and the app tells you when to try again. We may change these numbers, switch the AI features off for a time, or ask you to update the app before you can use them. Everything else in the app keeps working when the AI is off.

## AI results may be wrong

The AI reads bills and proposes changes to splits. It can misread a bill, miss or invent an item, take a price for the wrong item, or misunderstand what you asked. What it says may be false, incomplete or misleading, so do not rely on it without checking.

Before you ask anyone for money, check the items, the amounts, the total, who is on the bill and each person's share. The app shows the bill so that you can correct it, and changes proposed from a sentence apply only once you accept them. You are responsible for the amounts you ask other people to pay.

## Split Vibe holds and moves no money

Split Vibe is not a bank, a payment service or a money transmitter. It never holds, receives, sends or moves money, and it does not process payments.

- A payment request is a message that carries the way to get paid that you chose: a link, a QR code or details to type in. When the payer taps a link or scans a code, it opens their own banking or payment app, such as a bank app, PayPal or a UPI app, and the payment happens there, under that app's terms.
- We do not check that a link, a code or the details in a request are correct, or that they belong to the person who sent the request. Before you pay, check that you know who is asking and that the details are right.
- "Paid" in Split Vibe means only what people told the app: the payer says they paid, and the person who asked confirms it. Split Vibe does not see payments and cannot confirm, reverse or refund them.
- Debts and payments are between you and the people you split with. We are not a party to them, and we do not settle disagreements about them.
