Split Vibe

Delete your Split Vibe account

Deleting your account removes what Split Vibe's server keeps about you, apart from what is listed under What stays. It cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your Pro plan as Split Vibe knows it, and your purchase history at RevenueCat, which handles Pro purchases for Split Vibe.
  • Your place in groups. You show as "Former member", and every amount stays as it was, so nobody else's balance changes. A group you own passes to another member.
  • How to pay you, on the payment requests you made that are not confirmed yet.
  • The bill links you shared, which stop working, and what people picked on them.
  • The phones that get Split Vibe's notifications for you.
  • Your sign-in on every phone. A phone still signed in to the account is signed out: Split Vibe's server no longer accepts its sign-in, so it cannot use the account until you sign in again.

What stays

  • Bills, people and payment methods on your phone stay on your phone. To delete them too, delete your account in the app and tick "Also delete the bills, people and payment methods on this phone", or uninstall Split Vibe.
  • A Pro subscription goes on until you cancel it in Google Play: deleting your account does not cancel it. Manage your subscriptions on Google Play.
  • How many AI calls your account used this month, until the month ends, so that deleting the account and signing in again does not start them over.
  • When your account was deleted, for 30 days, so that phones still signed in to it are signed out.
  • Counts of what your account did in the last hour, until the next daily clean-up.
  • The bills and settlements of your groups, so that their balances stay right, and the payment requests between you and others, for the other person.

Delete your account

Sign in with the Google account you use in Split Vibe. You can still change your mind after that.
