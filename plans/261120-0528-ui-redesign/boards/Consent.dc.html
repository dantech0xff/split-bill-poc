Use the AI for your bills?

Split Vibe's AI is Claude, made by Anthropic. To use it, Split Vibe sends text through its own server to Anthropic in the United States, which works on it and sends back the answer.

Reading a bill sends the text this phone read from its photo, the instruction on how to split it, and the currency you count in. The photo stays on this phone.

Changing a split by a sentence sends what you typed or said, with the bill: the shop, its items, charges and amounts, the names of the people on it, yours too, how it is split and who paid.

You can take this back in Settings at any time. Without it, you type bills in by hand, and everything else in Split Vibe works the same.
